Our Story

It started with a simple frustration.

Getting laundry done has always been more complicated than it needs to be. You either know someone, rely on word of mouth, or just figure it out yourself. There was no easy, reliable way to find a quality dry cleaner near you, book them without the back and forth, and trust that your clothes would come back the way they left.

At the same time, talented dry cleaning businesses — people who are genuinely good at what they do — had no real way to reach new customers beyond their immediate neighbourhood. No platform. No visibility. No tools to manage orders professionally.

"What if finding a dry cleaner was as easy as booking a ride — and running one was just as straightforward?"

That question is what became Laundrify. A platform that connects customers who need their clothes taken care of with the skilled professionals who can do it — simply, reliably, and on both sides of the transaction.

We're just getting started. But the problem we're solving is real, the people we're building for are real, and we're in this for the long run.

Our Mission

Connect people with the services they need — and the tools to deliver them.

Laundrify exists to close the gap between the millions of people who need quality laundry services and the skilled professionals who provide them. We do that by building technology that makes the connection seamless, the transaction safe, and the experience worth coming back to — on both sides.

For Customers

Find verified dry cleaners near you, book in seconds, track your order in real time, and pay securely — all without a single phone call.

No more asking around, no more uncertainty, no more chasing people for updates. Just clean clothes, done properly.

For Dry Cleaners

Get discovered by customers in your area, manage your orders from a single dashboard, and receive weekly payouts directly to your bank account.

Focus on doing what you do best. Let Laundrify handle the rest — the bookings, the payments, the customer communications.

Be part of what we're building.
