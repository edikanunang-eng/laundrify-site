Community Guidelines

Laundrify works because people treat each other with respect. These guidelines keep the platform safe, fair, and genuinely useful for everyone.

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Every interaction on Laundrify — whether you're a customer booking a clean or a dry cleaner fulfilling an order — shapes the experience for everyone on the platform. These guidelines are not about rules for rules' sake. They exist because Laundrify is built on trust, and trust only works when everyone plays their part. Read them, live by them, and if you see something that doesn't belong here, tell us.

🤝

Treat everyone with respect

Every person on this platform — customer, dry cleaner, or support staff — deserves to be treated with basic human dignity. Disagree where you need to, but always with respect.

✅

Be honest

Say what you mean and mean what you say. Honest reviews, accurate listings, and genuine communication make the platform better for everyone and worse for no one.

🔒

Protect people's privacy

Don't share other people's personal information — their address, phone number, or payment details — outside of what's needed to complete a service.

🧺

Keep it on platform

The platform exists so both sides are protected. Taking transactions off Laundrify removes those protections for everyone — including you.

In-App Messaging

The chat feature exists to help customers and dry cleaners communicate about orders — special care instructions, pickup timing, item concerns. Keep it there.

✓ Do

  • Communicate clearly and promptly about your order
  • Ask questions about services before or during an order
  • Flag concerns about items (damage, stains, care needs) early
  • Be patient — dry cleaners are running a business and may not reply instantly
  • Be polite even when you're frustrated

✗ Don't

  • Use chat to harass, threaten, or abuse anyone
  • Share personal contact details to move transactions off the platform
  • Spam or send repetitive, irrelevant messages
  • Use offensive, discriminatory, or sexually explicit language
  • Advertise or promote any unrelated business or service
Heads up: In-app messages may be reviewed by Laundrify in the context of a dispute or complaint investigation. Don't say anything in chat that you wouldn't be comfortable having us read.

Reviews & Ratings

Reviews are one of the most powerful tools on Laundrify. A good review helps great dry cleaners grow. An honest negative review helps other customers make informed decisions. But only if reviews are genuine.

For customers

Your review should reflect your actual experience with the service. Be specific — what went well, what didn't, what could be better. A one-sentence review is less useful than a short, honest description. Rate the service as you genuinely experienced it, not based on unrelated factors.

✓ Good reviews

  • Describe your actual experience with the service
  • Mention specific things — turnaround time, item care, communication
  • Are fair even when negative — focus on the service, not the person
  • Are submitted promptly after receiving your order

✗ Not allowed

  • Fake, exaggerated, or fabricated reviews
  • Reviews submitted in retaliation for a pricing dispute or complaint outcome
  • Personal attacks on the dry cleaner as an individual
  • Reviews about things unrelated to the service (e.g., their location or appearance)
  • Threats to leave a bad review unless demands are met

For dry cleaner partners

You may respond to reviews professionally and factually. A calm, constructive response to a negative review often does more for your reputation than the review itself. What you may not do is post retaliatory reviews about customers, attempt to have legitimate reviews removed through false reports, or harass a customer who left an honest negative review.

Review manipulation is a serious violation. This includes incentivising positive reviews, coordinating fake reviews with friends or family, and filing false abuse reports against legitimate reviews. Violations may result in account suspension. See our Terms of Service for full details.

Respect & Inclusion

Laundrify is for everyone. We do not tolerate discrimination, harassment, or abuse in any form on our platform.

This means:

For customers: You may not refuse to be served by or discriminate against a dry cleaner based on their religion, ethnicity, gender, location, or any other personal characteristic unrelated to the quality of their service. You may not harass, threaten, or demean a Partner in chat, reviews, or any other part of the platform.

For partners: You may not refuse to accept an order from a customer based on discriminatory grounds. You may not harass, threaten, or abuse a customer, regardless of whether you believe their complaint is unfair. You have the right to decline orders for legitimate operational reasons — capacity, location, item type — but not on the basis of who the customer is.

Content that is racist, sexist, homophobic, religiously discriminatory, or otherwise hateful will be removed immediately and the account responsible will be subject to disciplinary action.

Keeping Transactions on Platform

This one protects you as much as it protects us.

When you pay or get paid through Laundrify, both sides are covered — customers have recourse through our refund process, and partners have their earnings protected by our payout system. The moment a transaction moves off platform, both sides lose those protections entirely.

For this reason, soliciting or agreeing to off-platform payments — whether it's a customer paying cash directly or a partner offering a "discount" if you book them directly — is a violation of these guidelines and our Partner Agreement.

If a Partner ever asks you to pay them directly or take future orders outside the app, please report it to us immediately. That Partner will be investigated and may be removed from the platform.

Safety

Everyone using Laundrify should feel safe. Physical, financial, and emotional safety all matter here.

During pickups and drop-offs

Interactions between customers and partners during item collection or return should be professional and safe. Do not engage in aggressive, threatening, or unsafe behaviour during any in-person interaction connected to a Laundrify order. If you ever feel unsafe during a Laundrify-related interaction, remove yourself from the situation and contact us and the relevant authorities.

Your account security

Keep your login credentials secure. Do not share your Laundrify account with anyone else. If you suspect your account has been accessed without your permission, contact us immediately at help@laundrify.com.ng and change your password right away.

Fraud & scams

Be cautious of anyone — whether claiming to be a customer, a partner, or even a Laundrify staff member — who asks you to make payments outside the app, share your password, or provide sensitive financial information. Laundrify will never ask for your password. If something feels off, trust that instinct and report it to us.

What Happens When Guidelines Are Broken

We want to be transparent about how we handle violations. The consequences depend on the severity and frequency of the behaviour.

⚠️

Warning

A formal notice for first-time or minor violations. We'll explain what happened and what needs to change.

⏸️

Suspension

Temporary removal from the platform for repeated or more serious violations. Duration depends on the offence.

🚫

Permanent Ban

Permanent removal for serious violations — fraud, harassment, identity abuse, or repeated offences.

Some behaviours — such as fraud, harassment, submitting false identity documents, or threatening another user — will result in an immediate permanent ban with no prior warning. In serious cases, we will report the matter to the relevant Nigerian authorities.

For the full enforcement framework, including the Partner strike system, see our Terms of Service and Partner Agreement.

See something? Tell us.

If you encounter behaviour that violates these guidelines — whether in chat, a review, or during a service interaction — please report it. We investigate every report and take action where guidelines have been broken. Your report may be the thing that protects the next person.

A Final Word

Laundrify is still growing, and you — whether you're a customer or a partner — are part of building what this community becomes. These guidelines will evolve as the platform does. If you ever have a suggestion for how we can make Laundrify a better, fairer, safer place, we genuinely want to hear it.

These Community Guidelines are supported by and should be read alongside our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, and Partner Agreement. In the event of any conflict between these guidelines and the Terms of Service, the Terms of Service shall prevail.
