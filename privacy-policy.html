6. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), we are required to have a lawful basis for each type of personal data processing we carry out. The lawful bases we rely on are:

Performance of a Contract

Most of our processing is necessary to perform the contract we have with you when you create an account and use our Services — for example, processing your order, connecting you with a partner, processing your payment, or sending your payout. Without this processing, we cannot provide the Service.

Consent

Where we rely on your consent — for example, to send you marketing communications or to access your precise location — we will ask for your explicit consent before doing so. You may withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your app settings or contacting us, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Legal Obligation

Some processing is necessary for us to comply with our legal obligations under Nigerian law, including retaining financial records for tax purposes and responding to lawful requests from public authorities.

Legitimate Interests

We may process your data where it is in our legitimate interests to do so, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests. This includes fraud prevention, platform security, improving our Services, and ensuring quality of service on the platform. We will always balance our legitimate interests against your rights before relying on this basis.

7. Payment Data & Paystack

All payment transactions on the Laundrify platform are processed by Paystack, a third-party payment processor licensed to operate in Nigeria. When you make a payment through the app, your payment card details are submitted directly to Paystack's secure servers and are not stored on Laundrify's systems.

We receive and retain the following payment-related data from Paystack for each transaction:

  • Transaction reference number
  • Transaction status (successful, failed, pending)
  • Amount paid and currency
  • Date and time of transaction
  • Masked card details (last four digits only, where provided by Paystack)

We retain this data for order records, dispute resolution, and compliance with Nigerian financial regulations. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or card expiry date.

Paystack's handling of your payment data is governed by their own Privacy Policy, which you can review at paystack.com/ng/privacy. By making a payment through our platform, you also agree to Paystack's terms and privacy policy.

8. Push Notifications

With your permission, we may send you push notifications through the Laundrify or Laundrify DryCleaners app. Push notifications may be used for the following purposes:

  • Order status updates (e.g., order accepted, ready for pickup, out for delivery)
  • New messages from customers or partners via in-app chat
  • Payment confirmation or payout notifications
  • Security alerts (e.g., new login detected)
  • Promotional offers and platform announcements (only where you have consented)

Push notifications do not give us access to your device. We only receive a confirmation of whether the notification was delivered or not.

How to manage push notifications

You can enable or disable push notifications at any time through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Notifications → Laundrify or Laundrify DryCleaners → toggle on or off
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Laundrify or Laundrify DryCleaners → Notifications → toggle on or off

Disabling push notifications will not affect order-critical communications, which we may also send via email or SMS.

11. Deleting Your Account & Data

You have the right to delete your account and request the deletion of your personal data at any time.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account from within the app:

  • Laundrify (Customer app): Go to Settings → Delete Account
  • Laundrify DryCleaners (Partner app): Go to Dashboard → Delete Account

You may also contact us at help@laundrify.com.ng to request account deletion.

What happens when you delete your account

  • All personal data associated with your account is permanently and immediately deleted from our active systems.
  • Residual copies in encrypted backup systems are purged within 30 days of deletion.
  • This action is irreversible. We cannot recover your account or data after deletion.
Important for Partners: Certain financial and transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years after account deletion as required by Nigerian tax law, even after your account is deleted. These records are retained in a minimal, secure form and are not used for any purpose other than legal compliance.

We strongly recommend resolving any outstanding orders, active disputes, or pending payouts before deleting your account, as we cannot process these after deletion.

16. Your Rights Under the NDPR

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we use it.

Right to Rectification

You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. See Section 11 for how to delete your account and data.

Right to Object

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis.

Right to Restriction

You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on your consent to process your data, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to complain to NITDA if you believe your data rights have been violated. See Section 17.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at help@laundrify.com.ng. We will respond to your request within 30 days. In some cases, we may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Please note that some rights are subject to limitations — for example, we may be unable to delete data we are legally required to retain (see Section 10).
