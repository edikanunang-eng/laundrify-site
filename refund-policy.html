Quick summary: Laundrify refunds are available only in serious, verifiable cases such as a lost item, clear damage caused by the cleaning process, a wrong item returned, or a paid service that was never rendered. Refunds are capped at the value of the service fee you paid — we do not cover the replacement value of garments. You have 48 hours from receiving your order to raise a complaint. All complaints are investigated before any refund is approved.

1. Our Approach to Refunds

Laundrify is a marketplace platform that connects customers with independent dry cleaning businesses ("Partners"). We are committed to maintaining high service standards on our platform, and we take complaints seriously.

However, because dry cleaning involves physical garments handled by independent third-party Partners, refunds are not automatic. Every refund request is reviewed individually and must meet the criteria set out in this policy before a refund is approved.

Refunds on the Laundrify platform are reserved for situations involving clear and verifiable fault — not general dissatisfaction or subjective quality preferences. We believe this is fair to both customers and the Partners who depend on the platform for their livelihoods.

This policy applies to orders placed through both the Laundrify customer app and services fulfilled by Partners using the Laundrify DryCleaners app.

2. Acceptance of Risk

By placing an order through Laundrify, you acknowledge and accept that dry cleaning and laundry services carry inherent risks. These include, but are not limited to:

  • Colour fading or bleeding in garments with unstable dyes
  • Shrinkage in garments not suited to the cleaning method applied
  • Reactions in garments with pre-existing wear, weakness, or undisclosed fabric sensitivities
  • Minor variations in folding, pressing, or finishing that do not materially affect the garment

These risks are inherent to the nature of laundry and dry cleaning services and are not automatically grounds for a refund. By using the platform, you agree that Laundrify and its Partners are not liable for outcomes that fall within these inherent risks, unless there is clear evidence of negligence or fault on the part of the Partner.

We strongly recommend that you disclose any special care instructions, fabric sensitivities, or pre-existing damage when submitting items. Failure to do so limits your ability to make a successful refund claim.

3. Eligible Refund Situations

A refund may be approved where there is clear, verifiable evidence of one of the following:

✓ May Qualify for a Refund

  • Your item was lost entirely and cannot be returned by the Partner
  • Your item was returned with visible damage clearly caused by the cleaning process — such as burns, tears, dye transfer from incorrect handling, or shrinkage from use of wrong temperature
  • You received the wrong item — garments belonging to another customer were returned to you
  • You paid for a service that was never rendered and your items were never picked up or processed
  • You were charged for a service you did not book (fraudulent or erroneous charge)

✗ Does Not Qualify for a Refund

  • General dissatisfaction with cleaning quality without evidence of fault
  • Pre-existing stains, damage, or wear that was present before submission
  • Outcomes resulting from undisclosed fabric type or care label instructions
  • Customer changed their mind after the order was placed or picked up
  • Minor imperfections in pressing or folding that do not damage the garment
  • Inherent risks of dry cleaning as described in Section 2
  • Items submitted in poor condition prior to cleaning
  • Complaints raised more than 48 hours after receiving the order
Important: Laundrify reserves the right to determine, at its sole discretion, whether a refund situation is eligible based on the evidence provided. We may request photos, order records, or other documentation to assess your claim. Approval is not guaranteed simply because a situation is listed above — the claim must be substantiated with evidence.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

In addition to those listed in Section 3, no refund will be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The complaint was not raised within 48 hours of receiving the completed order.
  • The customer is unable to provide reasonable evidence to support the claim (e.g., photos of the damage, description of the issue).
  • The damage or loss was caused by items left in pockets by the customer (e.g., pens, lighters, coins, keys) that were not disclosed before processing.
  • The garment contained prohibited or hazardous materials that the customer failed to disclose.
  • The customer provided false, misleading, or exaggerated information in the complaint.
  • The order was cancelled by the customer after pickup — cancellations are not permitted once an order has been accepted and collected (see Section 6).
  • The issue relates to an item that was already in a damaged, stained, or deteriorated condition before it was submitted for cleaning, and this was not disclosed at the time of submission.
  • The customer accepted the returned items without raising any objection at the time of collection.

5. Refund Amount & Cap

Where a refund is approved, the amount refunded will be equal to the service fee you paid for the affected order — that is, the amount charged by Laundrify for the specific service relating to the complaint.

Laundrify's maximum liability for any approved refund claim is strictly limited to the value of the service fee paid. We do not refund:

  • The market value, replacement cost, or sentimental value of any garment or item
  • Any consequential, incidental, or indirect losses arising from the service
  • Any costs incurred by the customer as a result of the item being unavailable (e.g., needing to buy a replacement outfit for an event)
Important: Laundrify is a technology marketplace platform, not an insurer or guarantor of garment value. We do not provide garment insurance. If you wish to protect the value of high-value items, we recommend using a specialist dry cleaner with their own insurance, or taking out a personal property insurance policy that covers garments sent for cleaning.

Where only part of an order is affected — for example, one item out of several is damaged — the refund will be calculated against the service fee attributable to that specific item, not the entire order total. Refunds are not partial in the sense of splitting between full and zero; once eligibility is confirmed for a specific item or service, the full relevant service fee for that item is refunded.

6. Cancellations

Orders placed through the Laundrify platform cannot be cancelled once they have been accepted and collected by a Partner. By placing an order, you agree to this no-cancellation policy.

No refund will be issued on the basis of a cancellation request after pickup. This is because the Partner will have already begun processing your items and has committed their time and resources to your order.

Before placing an order: Please ensure you are ready to proceed before confirming your booking. If you have questions about a Partner's services, pricing, or capacity, use the in-app messaging feature to communicate with them before placing your order.

7. How to Request a Refund

You must raise your complaint within 48 hours of receiving your completed order. Complaints raised after this window will not be considered, regardless of the nature of the issue.

To submit a refund request:

  1. Document the issue immediately. Take clear photographs of the damaged, lost, or incorrect item as soon as you receive your order. Do not wash, alter, or further handle the item before raising your complaint, as this may affect your claim.
  2. Contact Laundrify support within 48 hours. Email us at help@laundrify.com.ng with the subject line: "Refund Request — Order #[your order number]". Include your account email, the order number, a description of the issue, and your supporting photos or evidence.
  3. We acknowledge your request. We will send you a confirmation email acknowledging receipt of your complaint, usually within 1–2 business days.
  4. Investigation begins. We will review your complaint and contact the relevant Partner to gather their account of events. See Section 8 for the full investigation process and timelines.
  5. Decision is communicated. Once the investigation is complete, we will notify you of our decision by email. If your refund is approved, we will initiate the refund within the timeframes described in Section 9.

8. The Investigation Process

All refund requests are subject to a fair investigation before any decision is made. Laundrify acts as a neutral mediator in disputes between customers and Partners.

During the investigation:

  • We will review the details of your order, including pickup records, order notes, and any in-app communications between you and the Partner.
  • We will present the complaint to the relevant Partner and give them a reasonable opportunity to respond and provide their account of events.
  • We may request additional evidence from either party — including photographs, care label records, or written statements.
  • We will assess the claim against the eligibility criteria in Section 3 and reach a decision based on the balance of evidence.

Investigation timelines

Acknowledgement of complaint Within 1–2 business days of submission
Standard investigation period Within 14 working days of acknowledgement
Complex cases May be extended to 21 working days where additional evidence is required. You will be notified if an extension is needed.
Final decision Communicated by email. Our decision is final and binding within the scope of the platform.
Partners cannot cancel or withdraw from a dispute once it has been raised. All Partners are contractually required to cooperate with Laundrify's investigation process as a condition of using the platform. A Partner's failure to respond or cooperate will be taken into account when reaching our decision.

9. How & When You Get Paid Back

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed as follows:

Refund method Returned to your original payment method via Paystack reversal — the same card or account used to place the order.
Processing time 7–14 working days from the date the refund is approved. This window accounts for Paystack processing time and your bank's clearing period.
Notification You will receive an email confirmation when the refund has been initiated on our end. If you do not see the funds after 14 working days, contact us and we will follow up with Paystack on your behalf.
Note: Laundrify does not offer refunds as in-app wallet credits or cash payments. All refunds are returned exclusively via Paystack to your original payment method. If your original payment method is no longer active, please contact us before submitting your refund request so we can discuss an alternative.

10. Partner Liability

Where a refund is approved and the investigation determines that the fault lies with the Partner, the cost of the refund will be recovered from the Partner. Specifically:

  • The refund amount will be deducted from the Partner's next scheduled weekly payout.
  • If the deduction exceeds the Partner's pending payout balance, the outstanding amount will be carried forward and deducted from subsequent payouts until the full amount is recovered.
  • Partners are notified of any deductions made to their payout and the reason for the deduction.

Where the fault is unclear or cannot be conclusively attributed to the Partner, Laundrify reserves the right to absorb the cost of the refund at its discretion, in the interest of customer satisfaction and platform integrity. This does not constitute an admission of liability on the part of either Laundrify or the Partner.

Partners who accumulate repeated validated complaints or refund deductions may have their account suspended or permanently removed from the platform. For full details of Partner obligations and consequences, please refer to our Partner Agreement.

11. Fraudulent or Abusive Claims

We take fraudulent refund claims seriously. If we determine that a refund request was submitted in bad faith — including where a customer has provided false information, fabricated or manipulated evidence, or made repeated unfounded complaints — we reserve the right to:

  • Reject the refund request immediately
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the customer's account
  • Report the matter to the relevant Nigerian authorities where fraud is suspected
  • Pursue recovery of any costs incurred as a result of the fraudulent claim

Customers found to have submitted fraudulent claims will be permanently banned from the Laundrify platform.

For significant changes, we will notify users via in-app notice or email where required.
